# Bundled fonts

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which
requires the licence text to travel with the binaries. Add each upstream
`OFL.txt` beside these files before shipping a store build.

| Files | Family | Upstream |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `inter_regular.otf`, `inter_bold.otf` | Inter | https://github.com/rsms/inter |
| `sourceserif4_regular.otf`, `sourceserif4_bold.otf` | Source Serif 4 | https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif |
| `jetbrainsmono_regular.ttf` | JetBrains Mono | https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono |

The OFL permits bundling and redistribution in a compiled application. It does
not permit selling the fonts on their own, and any modified copy must be
renamed. Neither applies here — these are unmodified upstream releases.
